TITLE: GA-PubSub Pro — Submit a Query
================================================================================


HERO
----
PRO Queries

Have a question about licensing, your account, key renewal, or a technical
issue with GA-PubSub Pro? Submit your query here and we will get back to you.

Response time: 1–2 business days for Starter and Professional plans.
Enterprise customers receive same-day responses on business days.


WHAT THIS PAGE IS FOR
---------------------
Use this page for:

  Licensing questions
    - Which plan is right for my team or project?
    - Can I use PRO in a SaaS product I sell to others?
    - What does the commercial license allow and restrict?

  Purchase and pricing
    - How do I purchase a PRO license?
    - Do you offer volume discounts for larger teams?
    - Can I pay by invoice rather than card?

  License key issues
    - My key is showing an INVALID_SIGNATURE error.
    - My key expired — how do I renew?
    - I think my key was exposed — I need it revoked.
    - I need a key for a new deployment.

  Evaluation requests
    - I would like to try PRO before purchasing.
      (We issue short-term evaluation keys at no cost — just ask.)

  Technical questions (PRO-specific)
    - How do I configure the transport adapter for Redis?
    - How do I integrate multi-tenant namespaces with my existing architecture?
    - Is there a way to customise the authorization flow?


WHAT TO INCLUDE IN YOUR QUERY
------------------------------
To get the fastest, most useful response, include:

  Your name and company name (if applicable)
  Your plan (Starter / Professional / Enterprise / Evaluating)
  Your customer ID or account email if you are an existing customer
  A clear description of your question or issue
  For technical issues: ga-pubsub-pro version, runtime (Node.js version /
  browser), and the error message or code if relevant

AFTER YOU SUBMIT
----------------
You will receive a confirmation email at the address you provided.

A member of the team will review your query and respond within the timeframes
above. If your issue is urgent (for example, a compromised key or a production
outage caused by a key error), write URGENT in the subject line and we will
prioritise accordingly.

If you do not receive a confirmation email within 30 minutes, check your spam
folder or re-submit.


EXISTING CUSTOMERS — KEY RENEWAL
----------------------------------
If your license key is expiring or has expired, submit a renewal query with:
  - Your customer ID or account email
  - Your current plan
  - The number of months or years you want to renew for

We will issue a new key and send it to your registered email address.


NOT SURE WHICH PLAN TO CHOOSE?
-------------------------------
Starter:       Individuals and small teams, up to 3 production deployments.
Professional:  Growing teams, up to 25 deployments, up to 10 seats.
Enterprise:    Large organisations, unlimited deployments and seats,
               custom terms, SLA, audit rights, dedicated support.

If you are unsure, describe your use case in the query and we will recommend
the right plan for you.
