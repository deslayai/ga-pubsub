WHO THIS IS FOR
---------------
This page is for GA-PubSub Pro customers who need hands-on assistance beyond
a standard query. If you have a billing issue, a licensing dispute, an
enterprise onboarding request, or a production emergency, this is the right
place.

For standard licensing questions and key requests, use:
  deslay-ai.web.app/ga-pubsub-pro/queries

For general Core questions and community discussion, use:
  deslay-ai.web.app/community


WHAT WE HELP WITH
-----------------

Onboarding Assistance
Getting started with GA-PubSub Pro in an existing codebase? We can walk you
through the setup, help you configure transport adapters, and review your
initial integration to make sure everything is production-ready.

Account and Billing
  - Updating payment details or billing contact
  - Upgrading or downgrading your plan
  - Requesting an invoice or receipt
  - Cancellations and refund enquiries
  - Transferring a license to a different team or entity

License Management
  - Adding deployments to your plan
  - Adding seats (Professional / Enterprise)
  - Revoking and reissuing compromised keys
  - Managing licenses across multiple projects or environments

Enterprise Onboarding
Enterprise customers receive dedicated onboarding support, including:
  - Architecture review for your specific use case
  - Guidance on configuring multi-tenant namespaces at scale
  - Advice on transport adapter selection (Redis vs Kafka vs NATS)
  - Custom license terms if required by your legal or procurement team

Production Emergencies
If a license key issue is causing a production outage, contact us here and
mark your request as URGENT. Enterprise customers receive same-day response
on business days. For critical incidents outside business hours, include
URGENT in your subject line and we will respond as quickly as possible.

Audit and Compliance
Enterprise plans include audit rights (once per year with 14 days notice).
If your organisation requires a compliance review, license verification letter,
or security questionnaire response, contact us here.

ENTERPRISE CUSTOMERS
---------------------
Enterprise plan customers have access to:
  - Dedicated point of contact
  - Custom SLA terms (on request, included in Enterprise agreement)
  - Audit rights — once per year with 14 days notice
  - Custom license terms for unusual use cases or procurement requirements
  - Architecture guidance for large-scale deployments
  - Assistance with legal and security questionnaires

To discuss enterprise terms or pricing before purchasing, reach out here or
at the queries page and one of the team will contact you directly.


REFUND POLICY
-------------
If you are not satisfied with GA-PubSub Pro within 14 days of purchase,
contact us with your customer ID and reason. We will review your case and
issue a refund where appropriate. After 14 days, refunds are assessed on
a case-by-case basis.
